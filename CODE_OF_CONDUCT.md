# Gitgather Code of Conduct

Gitgather is a friendly hangout for people who build things on GitHub. Everything here happens in public GitHub Discussions, so **[GitHub's Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines) and [Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies) apply to everything you post.**

## Be a good neighbour
- Be kind, constructive and specific in feedback. Critique code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Keep posts about your own (or your team's) projects, and be honest about what they do.
- Use English or add a short English summary so more people can join in.

## Not allowed
- **Star-for-star, follow-for-follow or reaction trading**, "star my repo and I'll star yours", or any coordinated or automated inflation of stars, reactions, comments or followers. This is "inauthentic activity" under GitHub's AUP and also distorts the leaderboard.
- Spam, ads, referral links, crypto/NFT shilling, SEO link-dropping, or mass-posting.
- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, doxxing or sharing other people's personal data.
- Sexual or violent content, malware, phishing, credential-stealing tools or anything illegal.
- Impersonation of other people or projects.
- Multiple accounts or bots used to boost your own posts.

## Private chat
Private messages are visible only to the two participants, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. **Never send tokens, passwords or API keys.** Block anyone who bothers you.

## Reporting
- Use **Report** (⋯ menu) on any post, comment, profile or message on Gitgather — only admins can see reports.
- Or open an issue titled `Moderation:` with a link (don't repost the abusive content).
- For anything urgent or illegal, use [GitHub's report abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide, close, lock or delete content and block users from the repository. Typical steps: a reminder → hiding the post from the site → removing it → blocking the account from the repo. Severe violations skip straight to blocking and a report to GitHub.
