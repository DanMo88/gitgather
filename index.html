How the Top 100 works

Every hour a GitHub Action scores Showcase posts by activity in the last 24 hours: 3 pts per comment or reply (max 3 per person), 2 pts per person who reacts, 0.5 per reaction on comments, 0.5 per new star (max 10 stars), and +1 for fresh posts. The maker's own comments and reactions don't count. Conversation beats stars on purpose — no star-for-star trading, please; it's against GitHub's rules and those posts get removed.
